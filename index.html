Our Story

Assam met Maharashtra on a campus in Guwahati — and neither of them saw the rest of this story coming.

Chapter 01 Where Paths Crossed She grew up in Assam, Bengali roots and a quiet resolve, studying Biosciences at IIT Guwahati. He came from Maharashtra to study Design — different departments, different worlds, walking the same campus without a clue what was coming.
Chapter 02 Slowly, Something Shifted It wasn't sudden. It was conversations that ran later than they should have, jokes that made sense to no one else, and the quiet surprise of realizing one person's company had started to feel like home.
Chapter 03 Every Twist, Every Turn Two backgrounds, two languages, a hundred reasons it could've gone another way. Somewhere between the doubt and the what-ifs, they kept choosing to find out anyway.
Chapter 04 Miles Apart Then came the distance — different cities, different clocks, a love running on phone calls and stolen weekends. It tested everything they thought they knew about each other, and about themselves.
Chapter 05 Choosing Each Other, Again There were fights — real ones, the kind that come from caring too much to stay quiet. And every time, they found their way back to the same easy silence, choosing each other all over again.
Chapter 06 Every Milestone, Together Through deadlines and defenses, through days that felt impossible, they kept showing up for each other — cheering the wins, sitting through the hard ones, becoming each other's steadiest constant.
Chapter 07 Joy, Earned After the distance, the disagreements, the doubt — what's left is easy. Dancing under festival lights, laughing without trying, two people who fought for this and know exactly what it's worth.
Chapter 08 Now, We're Ready Assam and Maharashtra. Bengali and Marathi. Biosciences and Design. Two completely different stories that somehow always led here — to each other, and to the life they're about to begin.
